Conversational analytics agent · OTTO

Built for Dott. I designed it, built it and keep it running. Ask a question from Claude through its MCP server, or in the web UI, and get an answer drawn from the warehouse.

It opens quick analytics up to the whole company, no SQL needed. And it's more accurate than a generic LLM pointed at the same warehouse, because it's grounded in a knowledge base of Dott's actual metrics and business definitions: it answers the way the business defines things, not with a best guess.
